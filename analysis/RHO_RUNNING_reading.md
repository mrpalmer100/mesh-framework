# RHO-RUNNING: can the mesh's alpha run? (a reading, 2026-10-29)

The second of the three free tests of docs/CONSTANTS_AS_CONSTRAINTS.md (section 2, item 3), on the author's word.
A reading of registered material; no number of the mesh is compared with any measured value; nothing is

## 1. The behaviour a mechanism must reproduce

The electromagnetic coupling is screened: its effective value grows with the momentum transferred, logarithmically,
with a coefficient that counts the charged species that can be pair-created at that scale. This is not only a
collider fact. It is measured at the electron's Compton scale in atomic physics: the Uehling term of the Lamb shift
(about 27 MHz of the 1058 MHz 2S-2P splitting in hydrogen, with the opposite sign to the rest) is the vacuum
polarization of virtual electron-positron pairs at distances of order lambda-bar_C = 3.9e-13 m, and it is confirmed
to many digits in muonic hydrogen where it dominates. So a mechanism for alpha owes, at the first test, a vacuum
that polarizes at the Compton scale with the electron's mass as its threshold.

## 2. What the registry holds

The registered alpha is 1/alpha = 2 pi^2 rho^2 (ELEC-083) with rho a geometric ratio of mesoscopic lengths, the one
unknown g = l_q / a of FND-044. Charge is a winding number (FND-008), exactly quantized and conserved, with
reconnection exchanging exactly one 2 pi quantum (GRV-045) and net topological counts identity-protected through

log q^2), are unexplained (SM_EMERGENCE row 6).

## 3. Finding, by kind

A geometric ratio of lengths is of the right kind for alpha at zero momentum and of the WRONG kind for alpha as
measured, because a ratio of two fixed lengths cannot depend on the momentum of the probe. For rho to run, the
mesh would need, registered: (i) a vacuum in which winding-antiwinding pairs exist virtually, with an energy
threshold equal to twice the electron's mass, so that a winding's field is screened at distances inside
lambda-bar_C; (ii) those pairs behaving as spin-1/2 objects, so that the coefficient of the logarithm is the
Standard Model's and not a bosonic or classical one; (iii) the same for every other charged species at its own
threshold (muon, tau, the quarks with three colours), since the measured running from zero momentum to M_Z sums
them all. Item (i) is the unbuilt electron plus the imported quantum layer; item (ii) is row 6; item (iii) is rows
8 and 9. So the running of alpha is not a separate wall: it is gap 4 (the electron), the quantum layer and the
generations, seen from the coupling's side. A rho mechanism that produces 2.6348 and stops there has produced
alpha(0) and nothing about alpha, exactly the position Chain Theory is in (its chapter 147: the map from passage
count to momentum scale is open).

One sharpening the reading adds. The running is measured over distances from lambda-bar_C down to the collider
scale; at q = M_Z the probed distance hbar / q is about 2e-18 m, thirty times SMALLER than the registered strand
spacing a = 6.0e-17 m (FND-MATTER-044). Every measurement of alpha above about 3 GeV (hbar c / a) probes the mesh
below its own lattice, where the registered continuum description does not exist. This is FND-REL-004's tension
(the photon sector's dispersion bound a <= 9.3e-28 m against the matter sector's 6.0e-17 m) in a second,
independent form: the measured running of alpha between the Compton scale and M_Z is smooth and logarithmic across
the mesh's lattice scale with no feature at hbar c / a. Either the M-point spacing is not the spacing light and
virtual pairs see, or the lattice is invisible to screening for a reason the registry does not hold. Kept beside
FND-REL-004 as a second witness to the same tension, not resolved here.
