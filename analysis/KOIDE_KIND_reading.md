# KOIDE-KIND: what shape a generation mechanism must have (a reading, 2026-10-29)

The third of the three free tests of docs/CONSTANTS_AS_CONSTRAINTS.md (section 2, item 3), on the author's word.
A reading of a registered structural fact against the registry; no mesh number is compared with any measured
value, no phase or scale is fitted, nothing is registered by it.

## 1. The exact relation, stated as geometry

Koide's relation for the charged leptons, Q = (m_e + m_mu + m_tau) / (sqrt m_e + sqrt m_mu + sqrt m_tau)^2 = 2/3,
holds to a part in 1e5 for the pole masses. Written in the amplitudes s_i = sqrt(m_i), Q = |s|^2 / (s . n)^2 with
n = (1, 1, 1), so Q = 2/3 says exactly that the amplitude vector s makes an angle of 45 degrees with the diagonal
(1, 1, 1) in the three-dimensional space of generations: cos^2 = (s . n)^2 / (|s|^2 |n|^2) = 3/(2 x 3) = 1/2.
Equivalently (Brannen's form, checked algebraically here for arbitrary phase and scale): s_i = s_0 [1 + r cos(theta
+ 2 pi i / 3)], i = 0, 1, 2, gives Q = (1 + r^2 / 2) / 3 for every theta and s_0, so Q = 2/3 if and only if the
modulation ratio r = sqrt 2. The three masses are then the squares of one common amplitude modulated by a Z_3
phase pattern of fixed relative size sqrt 2, with two free quantities (the scale s_0 and the phase theta) for three
masses: one relation predicted, which is Koide's.

## 2. What this says about the KIND of mechanism

A generation mechanism of the right kind has these three properties, each of which is a yes-or-no question about
structure and none of which involves a number:
(a) mass is the square of an amplitude (mass = mode energy, with the mode's amplitude the thing the generations
    differ in);
(b) the three generations are three components of ONE object with a Z_3 (cyclic) structure, not three separately
    built objects;
(c) the Z_3 modulation has a fixed relative size, sqrt 2 against the mean, set by geometry rather than by a
    parameter.
A mechanism with (a) and (b) but a free modulation ratio r predicts nothing (it fits Koide with r). A mechanism that
builds three objects separately and lands on 206.77 has fitted, and Koide, which it does not produce, is the proof.
Note what the relation does NOT constrain: the phase theta (which sets the hierarchy, mu/e and tau/mu) and the scale
s_0 are free in the form; a mechanism must supply them separately, and a reading that "finds" theta by matching
the ratios is the numerology the house forbids. The phase is the open item, not the relation.

## 3. What the registry holds

(a) is registered: mass = mode energy (PM-005, Derived), the right form. (b) is not: PM-005 scopes generations 2 and
3 out, and the one attempt at the lepton ratios (PM-001, Failed and kept) used a "Koide phase" by hand with the
measured weak angle as input, which is exactly the fitting the relation exposes. But the registry holds one object
with the right symmetry for (b): FND-185's "S_3 families inside O_h," the finite family structure of the weave's
strand labels, whose Z_3 subgroup is the cyclic structure (b) needs; FND-185 reads it as the Weyl group of SU(3), a
shadow of colour, which is a different role for the same group. (c) has no registered candidate: nothing in the
registry fixes a modulation of sqrt 2 on anything.

## 4. Finding, by kind

The mesh has the right kind for (a) and a candidate symmetry for (b) in FND-185's S_3 families, which is more than
the constants page assumed. The missing piece is (c), a geometric reason for a modulation ratio of sqrt 2 on a Z_3
triplet, and the sqrt 2 is not a target to hit by search; it is a condition to be met by a mechanism built for
other reasons (a ratio of sqrt 2 is what a Z_3 pattern on a square lattice, or a 45-degree rotation, produces
naturally, which is a remark about kind and not a derivation). A generation proposal in the mesh should therefore
be a statement of the form "the three charged leptons are the three Z_3 phases of one S_3-family object whose
amplitude modulation is fixed at sqrt 2 by [a registered geometry]," and it should be declined on sight if it builds
the muon and tau as separate objects, because Koide says they are not.

## 5. What the constants page should say

Row "m_e, m_mu, m_tau": the mesh is of the right kind for (a) and holds a Z_3 in FND-185's S_3 families for (b);
(c) is the open condition; PM-001's failure is the registered example of the wrong kind (a phase inserted by hand).
Any three-generation charter cites this reading and states, before computing, which registered geometry fixes the
modulation ratio, with the phase theta left to the mechanism and never read from the masses.

PRED-001 (the neutrino mass sum, Modeled, T3) uses m_i = mu (1 + sqrt 2 cos(delta + 2 pi i / 3))^2 for the charged
leptons, with delta_c read from m_mu / m_e and the tau ratio then predicted to 0.01 percent, and for the neutrinos
with a declared pi / 12 offset. Section 3 above, which said no Z_3 triplet is registered, is wrong on that point:
(b) is in use; what is missing is (c), a geometric origin for the sqrt 2 and for the phases. See
analysis/PRED_001_confrontation_reading.md section 6.
