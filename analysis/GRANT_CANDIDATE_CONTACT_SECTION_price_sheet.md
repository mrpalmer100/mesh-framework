# GRANT-CANDIDATE-CONTACT-SECTION -- the cross-section-aware contact, priced for the author's desk
# (DRAFT 2026-10-04; a candidate, not a grant; the decision is reserved to the author)

## Why a candidate exists

Three roads stopped at one registered gap on 2026-10-04 (FND-183, ACTION-BRIDGE; the V_0 note in
analysis/ACTION_BRIDGE_results.md). FND-STRAND-002's chain gives every twist mode a mass through
an on-site orientation potential whose amplitude V_0 is a model input (GRV-072: the chain fixes
only kt/V_0); FND-STRAND-008 reads the twist band's gap as "exactly the strand mass scale" in
chain units; FND-182's charge-creation window and the route to a third firm discriminator (pin the
strand mass scale in eV, ELEC-101) both need V_0 in joules. FND-179 result B shows the registered
contact form V(r_line) has dV/dphi = 0 exactly: as registered, nothing in the mesh resists a
strand's azimuth at a crossing. A potential that is exact in a toy chain and absent from the
registered contact is a potential without a source. The natural source is the rope itself: the
registry's strand is a two-strand rope (the naming note of v3.31.0), whose cross-section is not
circular and therefore has a physical orientation where it presses on a neighbour. The registered
contact form is a function of the centre line only and cannot see that orientation. This sheet
prices the sentence that would let it.

## The candidate, stated in full

  GRANT-CANDIDATE-CONTACT-SECTION: at a crossing, the contact energy depends on the relative
  orientation of the two strands' cross-sections as well as on their centre lines:
  V = V_line(r) + V_sec(r) [1 - cos(n (phi_1 - phi_2 - phi_0))], with n the section's symmetry
  order (n = 2 for a two-strand rope: the section returns to itself under a half turn), phi_i the
  material azimuths, phi_0 the registered rest offset (0 or pi/n by symmetry), and V_sec(r) a
  contact amplitude that vanishes with the line contact (V_sec -> 0 as V_line -> 0; same range).
  The on-site potential of a strand threaded through a weave is then the sum over its crossings,
  V_0 = sum_contacts V_sec, with the period 2 pi/n in phi.

If granted, the imported sentences would number four: the pair is one thing; the weave is warm;
the quantum arrives whole; THE SECTION HAS A FACE.

## What it is and is not

  It IS one new term in the registered contact form, with one new amplitude and one symmetry
  order fixed by the rope's own structure (n = 2 is not a choice; it is what "two-strand" means).
  It is NOT a new primitive class: the contact already exists (the contact grant, FND-129); this
  adds a dependence the existing object was blind to.
  It does NOT touch the bulk strand dynamics (FND-179's total-derivative theorem for the twist
  flux stands), the photon, Maxwell, the dictionary, the Casimir results (FND-178 ran with the
  twist band gapped and would be re-read with the gap's value, not its existence, changed).
  It does NOT change the kink's existence or its force law (FND-181 holds for any V_0 > 0); it
  changes the kink's width w and the breakdown field's VALUE.
  Its period 2 pi/n = pi is a prediction, not a parameter: the sine-Gordon chain FND-STRAND-002
  used has period 2 pi; under this grant the registered chain's potential acquires period pi and
  the "one 2 pi of material twist" that a charge carries becomes TWO wells of the on-site
  potential, which is itself a testable structural consequence (a half-twist is a metastable
  state of a threaded strand).

## Scope discipline (what the grant may be used for, and nothing else)

  1. Sourcing V_0: the on-site orientation potential of a strand from its registered crossings.
  2. The strand mass scale in physical units: omega_min^2 = V_0/I_T with I_T = mu r^2 registered.
  3. Pinning w, and through it FND-182's window to a number (the discriminator route of ELEC-101).
  4. Nothing about charge transport through a terminal, magnetism, or the electron is granted.

## The price (four items, each on the face)

  P1. ONE NEW AMPLITUDE: V_sec(r) at a crossing, relative to V_line(r). The honest status at
      grant would be "bracketed by a derivation": for two pressed elastic rods of non-circular
      section the orientation-dependent part of the contact energy is of order the line contact
      times the section's aspect anisotropy, which the registered rod class (FND-118, one
      modulus) and the two-strand geometry (HBAR-005's d_c, the pair's pitch) determine up to an
      order-one factor. So P1 is one O(1) number, not a free scale.
  P2. ONE SYMMETRY ORDER: n = 2, fixed by the two-strand rope; stated as a derivation from the
      naming note, not chosen. If the author's rope has a different section, n follows it.
  P3. A REST OFFSET phi_0: 0 or pi/2 by the section's symmetry; a sign choice with a physical
      meaning (sections prefer to lie flat or crossed at a contact), decidable by the contact
      engine (test T-2 below), so a price only until the engine rules.
  P4. A RE-READ OF EVERY CLAIM THAT USED THE 2 pi PERIOD: FND-STRAND-002/008, FND-KIN-002,
      FND-181, FND-182 carry period 2 pi in phi; under n = 2 the period is pi and their chain
      units rescale. The force law F = 2 pi c_L eps' per winding is unchanged (it is the lock's
      torque times the winding's turn); the kink width and breakdown gradient change by factors
      the re-read computes. None of these claims is falsified by the grant; all acquire a rider.

## What it buys (the purchases, each a registered consequence if granted)

  B1. V_0 in joules from registered objects: V_sec summed over the registered crossing density
      (kappa_pack, the contact rule of FND-129). The strand mass scale omega_min in eV follows,
      and with it the twist-band gap FND-STRAND-008 predicted "falsifiably" but could not price.
  B2. FND-182's window collapses to a number: E_c with w fixed by V_0 and kt. A number below
      1.32e18 V/m and above every applied field is a firm discriminator candidate (census C1 met).
  B3. ACTION-BRIDGE's M2 and M3 unblock (a count and an action from the twist band), so the
      bridge question gets two live candidates where it had none. No promise they land.
  B4. The weave bath's gap (FND-STRAND-007/008) in physical units: a prediction for the lowest
      noise frequency a detector coupled to the vacuum can feel.

## Falsifiers (any one kills the candidate as stated)

  F1. The registered contact engine (the solver behind FND-129/EM-RECON-023), given two
      non-circular sections, finds NO orientation dependence of the contact energy at any
      pressing within the registered regime (V_sec = 0 to the engine's precision).
  F2. The orientation dependence exists but has period 2 pi (n = 1), not pi: the two-strand
      section does not behave as a two-fold object at a contact; the grant's n is wrong and
      the sentence must be re-stated or dropped.
  F3. V_0 as summed over registered crossings gives a strand mass scale that puts FND-182's
      E_c BELOW the laser record (about 1e15 V/m): the vacuum would already have broken down
      in applied fields. Kills the reading, not only the grant.
  F4. The half-twist metastable state (two wells per 2 pi) is incompatible with a registered
      topological fact (a charge must be one 2 pi, GG-006): if the registry forbids a stable
      half-winding, the pi period is excluded and n = 2 falls.

## Cheap tests, runnable before any adoption (sandbox, no machine time)

      at a registered depth, rotated through phi_1 - phi_2 in [0, 2 pi): read V(phi) and its

  T-2 (P3): the sign of the n = 2 term from the same sweep (flat or crossed preferred).

      T-1's amplitude is registered.

## Retirement clause

The grant retires into a theorem if the contact engine, run on the two-strand section with no
added term (T-1), produces the orientation dependence by itself from the rod class and geometry
already registered. Then V_sec is derived, not granted, and the sentence is deleted. T-1 is
therefore both the cheap test and the retirement test; it should run first.

## T-1 RUN (2026-10-04, sandbox; benchmarks/foundations/contact_section_t1.py; log analysis/CONTACT_SECTION_T1.log)

A correction to the sheet first: the registered contact engine cannot run T-1 as written, because
the registered form is V(r) = A_c/(1 + (r/sigma)^4) in the CENTRE-LINE separation and has no
section to rotate. The dependence the candidate names enters at the first step the registered
form skips: a section-aware contact acts on the SURFACE gap, r minus the two half-extents in the
contact direction, and a two-strand rope's half-extent is its support function
h(phi) = rho (1 + |cos phi|) (two discs of radius rho side by side). So T-1 ran on that geometry
with the registered contact law, circular sections recovering the registered form exactly.

Results (period, sign, amplitude), with rho/sigma the one unregistered ratio swept 0.05 to 0.5
and r/sigma swept 0.8 to 1.5:
  F2 (period): n = 2 in each azimuth at every point; the two-azimuth surface is separable with
      dominant harmonic (2, 2). The pi period is the two-strand section's own, as the sheet said.
  P3 (sign): the energy is lowest when the pair lies FLAT (minor radius facing the partner),
      so phi_0 = pi/2 from the pair axis; decided, no longer a price.
  P1 (amplitude): V_sec/V_line runs from 0.035 (rho/sigma 0.05, r/sigma 0.8) to 0.48
      (rho/sigma 0.5, r/sigma 1.5), roughly proportional to rho/sigma; the second harmonic is
      7 to 36 percent of the first (the |cos| support function is not a pure cosine). The one
      number P1 named is therefore rho/sigma: the strand radius against the contact range, both
      registered lengths in the engine's own units once the engine's sigma is identified with a
      registered length (HBAR-005's d_c is the candidate; not done here).
  F1: the dependence exists for any non-circular section with any contact law that acts on
      surfaces; it vanishes only if the contact acts on centre lines, which is the registered
      convention and not a physical statement.

Reading for the desk: the grant's content is one sentence smaller than priced. "The section has
a face" reduces to "the contact acts on surfaces, not centre lines" plus the two-strand section
already registered; n = 2 and phi_0 then follow by geometry (T-1), and V_sec/V_line follows from
rho/sigma. The retirement clause is half met: the ORIENTATION DEPENDENCE is derived, not granted;
what remains a grant is the surface-contact convention itself (the registered form's centre-line
convention was a modelling choice, EM-RECON-023, never a derivation). The price falls to P1 as a
ratio of two registered lengths and the P4 re-read. Recommendation unchanged in direction,
stronger in force: adopt the surface-contact convention or decline it; T-1 leaves nothing else
to test cheaply. If adopted, the chain to V_0 in joules is: V_sec per crossing at the registered
pressing times the registered crossing density, then omega_min^2 = V_0/I_T. That chain is a
commission, not a cheap test, and it ends in FND-182's number.
