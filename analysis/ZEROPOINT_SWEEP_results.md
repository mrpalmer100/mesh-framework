# THE ZERO-POINT NAMING SWEEP (2026-09-27, sandbox; the FND-097 rename-sweep precedent)

FND-178 registered that the corpus uses "zero-point" for two objects: (A) the SPECTRAL
zero-point, the FND-109 ledger's half-sum over carried modes, the object Sakharov induction
(GRV-021/025) and the Casimir force act on, carrying hbar by import; and (B) the BUDGET
zero-point, the dynamical share of the vacuum's energy bill (FND-MATTER-041's window,
FND-132's identity, Prediction 32). CASIMIR-F3 showed (B) does not produce what (A)
produces. This sweep walks the registry and the docs, tags every use, binds the two names
in NAME_REGISTRY, and applies the qualifier where the front door uses the word.

## Method

Every claim whose title or note contains "zero-point" or "ZPE" (71 of 775) was tagged by
the context words around the term (spectral markers: half-sum, mode, band, Sakharov,
Casimir, regulator, cutoff, spectrum, hbar; budget markers: share, budget, bill, window,
dynamical, rotation, winding, fraction, the 0.615/0.779/0.889 numbers). A claim with both
kinds in comparable measure is MIXED and its review is owed; the tag is a reading aid,
not a rider, and registered text is history and is not edited (NAME_REGISTRY rule).

## Counts

49 spectral, 8 budget, 14 mixed (review owed), of 71 claims.

## Binding names (entered in docs/NAME_REGISTRY.md)

- "zero-point energy (spectral)" or "the spectral zero-point": the FND-109 half-sum over
  carried modes, lattice-regulated, hbar by import (GRV-014). Owners: GRV-021, GRV-025,
  FND-109, FND-178, the Casimir ledger, FND-BOUND-001's fence ("quantum zero-point / shell
  structure" is this sense).
- "zero-point share (budget)" or "the budget zero-point": the dynamical share of the vacuum
  energy bill, excess over booked / total, [0.615, 0.779] re-priced to the level-1 edge plus
  rider (FND-169). Owners: FND-MATTER-041 (the window), FND-131/132 (the identity), Prediction
  32. No symbol (NAME_REGISTRY's standing rule for the share).
- Rule from today: new text uses the qualifier whenever the sense is not fixed by the
  sentence; "zero-point" alone is reserved for the spectral sense, which is the majority
  and the standard-physics meaning.

## Review closed (2026-10-04, v3.32.0 cut)

The 14 MIXED claims were read one by one. Thirteen use 'zero-point' in the spectral sense
(the GRV-004 tower and its Sakharov residual: EM-RECON-016, GRV-005, FND-038, FND-041, FND-042;
the knot zero-point instrument and its lever: FND-MATTER-009/011/057/064; the nuclear kinetic
zero-point omission: NUC-009; vibrational zero-point corrections: CHEM-DYN-002; the FND-109
convention: FND-119, FND-123) and carry a naming rider saying so. ELEC-101 names both senses on
purpose and carries a rider saying that. No claim was found to use the budget sense without
saying so. The sweep's tags are now 62 spectral, 8 budget, 1 both-by-design.
