## The theorem as Lean-verified (openai/math, lean/docs/090.md at commit adc7f124)
"The density-one triangular lattice minimizes lower energy per particle among all locally finite planar
configurations of centered-disk density one, for every nonnegative completely monotone function of squared
distance. Infinite energies are allowed in the comparison." Comparator statement TriangularEnergy.lean. The
manuscripts (four) also give the sharp Gaussian minorants and the planar circle-packing certificate; the
uniqueness of periodic equality cases is outside the verified statement.

So the hypotheses are: (H1) two dimensions; (H2) a pair energy f(r) that is a completely monotone function of
u = r^2 (every derivative in u alternates in sign, which by Bernstein's theorem means f is a nonnegative mixture
of Gaussians exp(-t r^2)); (H3) comparison at fixed density among all locally finite configurations.

## The registered open question (FND-095/096)
The bundle count n_b of a tube's constituent strands is OPEN pending a non-engine determination; Chain A's

verbatim) places the strands on a hex spiral in the cross-section with the contact energy

(the kappa50/kappa250 floor cells), not two lattice symmetries: both use the hex arrangement.

## Does 090 bear on it?
Not on the count. The open question is how many constituents, decided by the pin m_b = [63.0, 73.4] and the
measured currency r_inf; a theorem about which arrangement minimizes energy says nothing about n_b.

On the arrangement, which the engine ASSUMES: 090 would turn "hex assumed" into "hex forced" if the registered
contact form satisfied H2. It does not. With u = r^2, f = 1 / (1 + u^2 / sigma^4) has
    f''(u) = 2 sigma^4 (3 u^2 - sigma^4) / (sigma^4 + u^2)^3,
which is NEGATIVE for r < sigma / 3^(1/4) = 0.76 sigma (f'' at u = 0 is -2 / sigma^4). A completely monotone
function has f'' >= 0 everywhere. The registered contact form is a soft-cored repulsion (flat at r = 0, an
inflection at 0.76 sigma) and is not a mixture of Gaussians; the theorem's hypothesis fails exactly in the
region the engine's bundles occupy (pitch = sigma + gap, so nearest neighbours sit at 1.05 sigma and 1.80 sigma,
on both sides of the inflection). Soft-cored potentials are the class for which triangular optimality is known
to FAIL in general (they can favour honeycomb, square or clustered phases at some densities), so the gap here is
not a technicality.

## Verdict of the reading
DOES-NOT-MATCH. Family 090 does not touch n_b, and it does not justify the engine's hex arrangement, because the
registered contact form is not completely monotone in squared distance. The hex arrangement in tav2/tav3 remains
an instrument assumption, as FND-077/079 already carry it.

## What would make it match (not proposed, recorded)
If a future registration of the inter-strand interaction in a bundle's cross-section were of Gaussian-mixture
form (for instance a tension-mediated repulsion exp(-r^2 / w^2), or an inverse power of r), 090 would force
hex by theorem for that interaction at any density, and the "hex" cell of the packing windows would be the only
cell. That is a statement about a form the registry does not hold today.
