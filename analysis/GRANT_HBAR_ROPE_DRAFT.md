## The proposition (what is granted)
The quantum of action is carried at the ROPE level. One rope of n_rs coarse strands, moving coherently as a single
transverse standing wave at the strand amplitude a, carries the action S_rope = n_rs S1, where S1 = pi T0 a^2 / 2c
is the registered strand action quantum (HBAR-003's standing-wave form) and the n_rs follows from tension
additivity (QGATE-005, T_rope = n_rs T0, derived from mu = T / c^2). The grant sets S_rope equal to the measured
quantum and thereby SETS n_rs, the one underived integer above the anchor in FND-110's ladder:

    DECISION 1 (the author's): the quantum is
      (A) hbar:      n_rs = hbar / S1 = 12,882 at the M-point;  R = a sqrt(n_rs) = 113 a = 6.8 fm
      (B) hbar / 2:  n_rs = 6,441;                                R = 80 a = 4.8 fm
    The HBAR series compared S1 with hbar (HBAR-003: hbar = 3549 S1 at its parameters; 12,882 at the M-point); the
    electron's spin is hbar / 2. The two differ by exactly the factor the registry has not derived (HBAR-003's own
    finding that action quantization and spin quantization are one problem). The grant must pick one and say so.

What the grant buys: the rope level becomes anchored (FND-110's ladder has two fixed rungs instead of one); the rope
radius R = a sqrt(n_rs) is a number; EM-RECON-008's missing input r_w / xi becomes computable once xi is; HBAR-005's
mesoscopic patch (L_hbar = 4.3 fm) is identified as the rope's own cross-section rather than a coherence length in
the medium; the hbar wall is converted from "no mechanism bridges S1 to hbar" (ACTION-BRIDGE, FND-183) into a
packing question, "why a rope has n_rs strands", which is FND-MATTER-003's kept obstruction (charge counts winding,
not packing) now carrying a number.
