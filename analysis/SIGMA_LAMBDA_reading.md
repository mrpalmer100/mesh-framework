# SIGMA-LAMBDA: does the medium's vacuum energy gravitate? (a reading, 2026-10-29)

The first of the three free tests of docs/CONSTANTS_AS_CONSTRAINTS.md (section 2, item 3), on the author's word.
A reading of registered material; no commission, no number of the mesh is compared with any measured value;
the registration of its finding as a rider is proposed at the end for the author's decision.

## 1. The question, as the constants page put it

The registered vacuum carries Sigma = 3.6e35 J/m^3 (FND-030), and the cosmological constant is a cancellation or an
absence to 120 decimal places in Planck units. A mechanism that computes Lambda from its own vacuum energy is wrong
unless it says why that energy is not a source. The constants page found no registry entry saying why Sigma does
not gravitate. That was a miss on the page's part: the registry has the entry, and also a second entry that
pulls the other way. This reading sets the two beside each other.

## 2. The static face: Sigma does not gravitate, by theorem

GRV-004 (ZERO-POINT THEOREM, Modeled): "given the framework commitment that gravity is the effective metric of
transverse-sector excitations (GRV-001), a uniform network of ANY tension density is flat: rest tension does not
gravitate; only deviations do." The mechanism behind it is GRV-005's: the source of the conditioning field is the
divergence of the stress, div(stress) = -f, and a uniform stress has no divergence. The 1/r field is forced by
three-dimensional elastostatics, sourced by stressed defects (mass-knots), and a homogeneous prestress of any size is
the zero point. EM-RECON-029 registers the consequence as a quantified interpretation ("the uniform medium must NOT
gravitate; the GRV induced-gravity route is the registered position: only defects and gradients source"), and
QGATE-009 names the one caveat: a medium denser than its densest excitations must still be shown to condition the
way the weak-field derivation requires, in any strong-field or cosmological extension.

So on this face the answer to the test is no, and it is no by construction rather than by cancellation: Sigma is
not a term that is cancelled against another; it is a uniform stress, which is not a source of the elastostatic
field at all. The 45 orders between Sigma and the observed vacuum energy density are not a problem for this face,
because Sigma was never a candidate for Lambda.

The same theorem covers the bath. FND-BOUND-001 reads the mode bath's zero-point stress as "a structured
deviation spectrum," structured in mode space; its MEAN stress in a homogeneous isotropic medium is a uniform
isotropic tensor, which adds to the prestress and is flat by GRV-004 for the same reason. Only the bath's spatial
gradients (near a boundary or a defect: the Casimir and contact effects) are sources. On the static face there is
no induced cosmological constant to address.

## 3. The induced face: the m^3 locus, registered and unaddressed

GRV-095 reads the corpus's weak-field gravity as "the derived induced channel": integrating out the mesh's mode bath
produces an Einstein-Hilbert tension zeta D hbar c / a^2, and the fork between scale selections is resolved to
F-SAK, a = 8 Planck lengths, from which G is read (GRV-006/075/095). This is Sakharov's induced gravity in the
mesh's clothing. In every induced-gravity programme the same integration that produces the a^-2 (Einstein-Hilbert)
term produces an a^-4 (volume, cosmological) term one order lower in the expansion, and GRV-028 and GRV-029 say so
on their faces: "the induced m^3 volume term, the cosmological-constant locus, remains the standing unaddressed
problem it is in every induced-gravity programme"; GRV-029's unconditional table inherits it as a named standing
item. In Planck units that term is of order a^-2 with a = 8 l_P, i.e. of order 1e-2, against an observed value of
order 1e-122: the full 120-order problem, on this face, exactly as in field theory. (The scale of the induced term
is stated so its kind is clear; it is not a prediction of the mesh for Lambda, which is the point of section 4.)

Note that Sigma is not this term. Sigma = 3.6e35 J/m^3 is the medium's registered stiffness, about 74 orders below
hbar c / a^4 at a = 8 l_P; the induced volume term is the zero-point of the mode bath at the Planck-class cutoff,
not the medium's tension. The constants page conflated the two; this reading separates them. The test "does Sigma
gravitate" is answered no on both faces. The live question is whether the BATH'S zero-point gravitates, and the two
faces answer it differently.

## 4. The reconciliation, and what it costs

The two faces are two descriptions of one medium, and they must agree. The elastostatic face says the bath's mean
zero-point stress is uniform and therefore flat (GRV-004 applied to it). The induced face writes an effective
action in which the same zero-point appears as a volume term that curves. The resolution is forced by the
dictionary, not chosen: GRV-029's metric is built from the four coefficient functions (mu, T_x, T_y, T_z) of the
transverse wave operator, and a uniform additive shift of T is, by GRV-004, a flat metric (a global rescaling of
c_local = A/B with no gradient). The induced action's volume term is that uniform shift. So under the registered
dictionary the a^-4 term of the induced expansion is NOT a cosmological constant; it is the prestress the metric
is defined relative to, and it curves nothing. The induced face inherits GRV-004 the moment its effective action
is read through GRV-029's dictionary rather than through the field-theory habit of reading a volume term as Lambda.

This is a reading of two registered claims against each other, and it is a theorem-shaped statement that nobody
has written: that the induced channel's cosmological-constant locus is discharged by the zero-point theorem under
the registered metric dictionary. It has a price and a prediction, both of which are of the KIND the constants
page asks for:

- The price: it works only if the bath is homogeneous and isotropic at the scales where the metric is defined. Any
  spatial structure of the zero-point stress (a boundary, a defect, a gradient of the local cutoff a) IS a source,
  and the Casimir and contact effects are its registered forms. The medium must be uniform on cosmological scales
  for Lambda to vanish on this face; and the QGATE-009 caveat (a medium denser than its excitations conditioning as
  required) is unchanged.
- The prediction, of kind only: a uniform static medium has Lambda = 0 exactly. The observed cosmological term is
  then NOT a vacuum energy; it must be a DEVIATION at cosmological scale, which in the registered vocabulary means
  a global gradient of the medium's tension or a slow drift of it in time. The registry already carries one such
  channel: PRED-003's coupling drift runs through the tension (d ln alpha = -2 d ln G through T at fixed kappa and
  a), and HBAR-007 names the standing alternative that the spacing is fixed by history rather than by equilibrium.
  A drifting tension is a relaxation mechanism, and a relaxation mechanism gives an equation of state that is not
  exactly w = -1. That is the kind of statement the constants page files under Lambda: the mesh belongs to the
  class "vacuum energy does not gravitate by construction, and the observed term is a relaxation," not to the class
  "a cancellation." Whether w departs from -1, and by how much, is a computation nobody has chartered, and the
  no-hunt clause applies to it as to everything else.

## 5. Finding

Under the registered dictionary (GRV-029) and the zero-point theorem (GRV-004), the mesh's vacuum energy does not
gravitate, on either face: Sigma is a uniform prestress (flat by theorem), and the induced channel's a^-4 volume
term, the m^3 locus GRV-028/029 flagged as unaddressed, is the same uniform prestress read in the effective
action's vocabulary, hence also flat. The cosmological-constant locus is discharged for a homogeneous medium, at
the cost of requiring homogeneity on cosmological scales, and with the consequence that any observed cosmological
term is a deviation (a tension gradient or drift), not a vacuum energy. The constants page's row for Lambda should
read: kind, "does not gravitate by construction; the observed term a relaxation"; registered primitive, GRV-004
with GRV-029's dictionary; open, the drift's equation of state.
