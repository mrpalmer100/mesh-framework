## VERDICT: STANDOFF-UNREGISTERED, with the question restated

The reads found that the quantity CROSSING-PRICE named, the standoff of two crossing strands at a
node of the vacuum weave, is not a registered object because the registered weave does not have
pressing crossings at all. Restated in the registered picture, the on-site orientation potential
can only come from the JUNCTIONS, and whether a junction locks the two-strand rope's material
azimuth, and with what fraction of the junction's registered director locking, is unregistered.
The arithmetic then says what fraction the FND-182 reading needs, and it is a very small one.

G2 Crossings away from nodes do not interact. The registered picture (rope_weave_universe,

G3 The two-level winding (FND-091's angles and pitch) is the geometry INSIDE a coarse strand

   closest approach between two coarse strands.

So G4 returns no standoff. The three readings the charter offered (touching, spaced,
intermediate) all presuppose a crossing that presses, and the registry has none. The commission
therefore restates the object: the on-site orientation potential V_0 of a strand threaded through
the weave is the sum over its JUNCTIONS of whatever part of the junction's locking depends on the
rope's material azimuth. FND-001's J is the director part; the azimuthal part under the fourth
grant (a tie that holds a non-circular section's face) is not registered. Call its fraction of
J "f". That is the one number, and it is a different number from the one CROSSING-PRICE named.

Reading: a junction that locks the azimuth at any ordinary fraction of its director locking
(f of order 0.1 to 1, which is what tying a flattened two-strand rope at a node would do) gives a
charge-creation field of order 1e21 to 1e22 V/m, a thousand times above the Schwinger field,
and a self-trapped charge-kink (w of order 0.002 to 0.01, far below the 0.8 coasting onset).
The FND-182 discriminator reading, and separately the registered coasting regime of the kink,
both need f between about 1e-7 and 1e-4: an azimuthal locking a thousand to a million times
weaker than the director's. No registered geometry supplies a fraction of that size, and the
two-strand section's anisotropy argues for the opposite (T-1: V_sec/V_line of 0.04 to 0.5 for a
surface contact). The coincidence CROSSING-PRICE recorded (the coasting regime inside the
discriminator window) survives the restatement unchanged: both demand the same tiny f.

## What this means, stated plainly for the desk

The fourth grant did what it was adopted to do: it gave V_0 a source. Walking the chain from
that source to FND-182's number has now located the source at the weave's junctions, where the
registry already has an exact locking energy for the strand's direction and nothing for its
azimuth. If the junction locks the azimuth the way it locks the direction, the mesh vacuum does
not break down below QED's field and the charge-kink does not coast; FND-182's reading would then
be ABOVE-QED (no discriminator) and FND-181's coasting regime would need a rider. If the junction
barely locks the azimuth (f ~ 1e-6), the reading is a discriminator. Nothing registered decides
between those, and the commission does not. What it does say is that the honest prior is not
with the tiny fraction, and that the question is now a precise one about the node: how does a
tie that fixes a two-strand rope's direction treat the rope's face?
