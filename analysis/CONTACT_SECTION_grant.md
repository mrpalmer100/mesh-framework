## The grant, stated in full

  GRANT-CONTACT-SURFACE: at a crossing, the contact energy is a function of the gap between the
  two strands' SURFACES, not of the distance between their centre lines. With the registered
  contact law V(.) = A_c/(1 + (./sigma)^4) (EM-RECON-023) this reads

      V = A_c / (1 + ((r - [h_1(phi_1) + h_2(phi_2) - 2 h_bar]) / sigma)^4),

  where r is the centre-line separation, h_i(phi_i) the half-extent of strand i's cross-section
  in the contact direction when its material azimuth is phi_i, and h_bar the orientation
  average of h, so that a circular section (h constant) recovers the registered form exactly.
  The strand's section is the registered two-strand rope's: two discs of radius rho side by
  side, h(phi) = rho (1 + |cos phi|) (the support function), period pi.

The imported sentences now number four: the pair is one thing; the weave is warm; the quantum
arrives whole; THE CONTACT ACTS ON SURFACES.

## What it is

One convention replaced by another. The centre-line convention was a modelling choice made when
the contact grant (FND-129, class C) was built; it was never derived from the postulates. The
surface convention is also a choice, and it is the one every physical contact obeys: the thing
that presses is the skin. It adds no constant (rho and sigma are registered lengths; h_bar is
fixed by rho), no force, no mechanism. T-1 (benchmarks/foundations/contact_section_t1.py) shows
that once it is adopted the orientation dependence, its period (pi: the two-lobed section
returns to itself under a half turn), its sign (the pair lies flat, minor radius toward the
neighbour) and its amplitude (V_sec/V_line roughly proportional to rho/sigma, 0.035 to 0.48 over
the swept range) all follow by geometry. Nothing in them is chosen.

  B1. The on-site orientation potential V_0 acquires a registered source: the sum over a
      strand's crossings of the orientation-dependent part of V. The potential FND-STRAND-002's
      kink lived in, and GRV-072 recorded as fixed only in ratio, becomes computable in joules.
  B2. The twist band's gap, "exactly the strand mass scale" in chain units (FND-STRAND-008),
      acquires a value in physical units: omega_min^2 = V_0 / I_T.
  B3. FND-182's charge-creation window collapses to a number once w follows from V_0 and kt
      (the route ELEC-101 named for a third firm discriminator).
  B4. ACTION-BRIDGE's candidates M2 and M3 (FND-183) unblock.
  B5. The weave bath's gap (FND-STRAND-007/008) gets a frequency a detector could be asked about.

## What it does NOT buy (stated before anyone asks)

  It does not spin a strand from the bulk (FND-179's total-derivative theorem stands). It does
  not supply a terminal (GRANT-CANDIDATE-TERMINAL stays parked). It does not touch the photon,
  Maxwell, the dictionary, the Casimir results (FND-178 assumed the twist band gapped; the gap's
  VALUE changes, its existence does not). It does not pin w, kt or V_0 by itself; B1 to B3 are
  commissions with bars, and their numbers are not known today.

## The period re-read (owed; riders applied on adoption)

  FND-STRAND-002, FND-STRAND-008, FND-KIN-002, FND-181 and FND-182 carry an on-site potential of
  period 2 pi in the material azimuth. Under the two-strand section the period is pi: a charge's
  one 2 pi of material twist (GG-006) now spans TWO wells of the on-site potential, and a
  half-twist is a metastable orientation state of a threaded strand. Pre-adoption read (F4 of the
  price sheet): GG-006's integer is the LINKING number of two strand curves, conserved and
  integer; a half material twist of one strand about its own axis is twist, not linking, and the
  registry forbids nothing about it (no claim mentions a half twist at all). F4 did not fire.
  The riders say: the chain units of these claims rescale under period pi; the force law
  F = 2 pi c_L eps' per winding is unchanged (the lock's torque times the winding's turn); the
  kink width and the breakdown gradient are re-read when V_0 is priced (commission B1 to B3).

## Falsifiers (the grant is a claim about the world, and can die)

  F1. The chain to V_0 (B1) gives a strand mass scale that puts FND-182's E_c below the laser
      record (about 1e15 V/m): the vacuum would already have broken down in applied fields.
      Kills the reading and the grant together.
  F2. A registered state that requires the centre-line convention to exist (a gated member whose
      contact bookkeeping changes under the surface gap by more than its own gate) is found: the
      convention change is then not inert on the archive and must be re-adjudicated.
  F3. The half-twist metastable state is shown, by a registered engine, to be dynamically
      unstable at every registered pressing: the period-pi potential has no second well in
      practice and the two-strand section does not behave as two-lobed at a contact.
  F4. The orientation dependence computed on a full two-strand rope engine (not the support
      function) has a different period from pi: the support-function model misread the section.

## The austerity cost, logged without softening

  Four imported sentences where there were three. The count of underived conventions does not
  rise (one convention replaced one convention), but the honest statement is that the mesh's
  contact law was never derived either way and now carries a second explicit choice on its face.
  The retirement clause: the grant retires into a theorem if a strand-level engine that models
  the two-strand rope as two strands, with the pair's own contacts and no section bookkeeping,
  reproduces the period-pi orientation dependence by itself. That engine is the strand-level
  campaign's Phase 2b (twist-writhe coupling, named in FND-STRAND-002) and does not exist yet.
