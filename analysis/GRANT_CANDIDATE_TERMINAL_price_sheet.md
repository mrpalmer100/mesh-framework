## Why a candidate exists

FND-179 proved that in the registered screw-stretch sector the torque on a strand's azimuth
is the derivative of a twist flux, J = lambda Phi' + c_L u', so a steady EMF cannot spin a
conductor from the bulk; a steady rotation needs terminals that source and sink angular
momentum. FND-180 tested the one registered event that looked like such a terminal, the
reconnection, on its own engine, and found it moves 0.03 of a turn, not 2 pi. The registry
therefore holds NO object that can do what "current is spin" requires. Under the standing
rule that ontological commitments are the author's, the missing object is written here as
a candidate with its price, its purchases, its falsifiers and its retirement clause, in the
form the contact grant (FND-129) and the third grant (FND-STRAND-025) used.

If granted, the imported sentences would number four: the pair is one thing; the weave is

## What it is and is not

  It IS a bookkeeping statement at the boundary of a conductor: one charge in, one turn in.
  It is NOT a mechanism: it does not say what physical process at an electrode performs the
  handover (the registered reconnection does not, FND-180), nor what the battery's stored
  winding reservoir is. Those are the named next-orders below, exactly as the funneling
  mechanism stayed open under Grant 3.
  It does NOT touch the bulk: FND-179's total-derivative theorem stands; the bulk still
  cannot source rotation. It does NOT touch the photon, Maxwell, the dictionary, or the
  Casimir results. It adds no coupling constant: the 2 pi is the topological unit charge
  already carries.

  B1. Current = phase advance rate, exactly: I = e/(2 pi) x d(Phi)/dt at any cross-section.
