## Why

The only thing stopping GRV-BLIND-2 is the seed. Everything else is frozen and already credentialed: unit calib passed C-1 to C-4 (bars reproduced to 0.180044/0.632099, predictions byte-identical, GRV-BLIND-1's draw reproduced at 155 eligible), the predictor (benchmarks/gravity/grv_blind_1.py) and the draw/seal/verdict tool (tools/holdout_galaxies.py) are called unchanged, the bars are a locked rule computed from training galaxies only, the floors F-1 and F-2 gate the seal, and the verdict script's forms are fixed. A seed from a committed future drand round changes none of that. It is chosen by nobody, cannot be known to the author or the lab before the round is published, and is publicly verifiable afterwards, which is the property the charter's 'the author does not see the seed before the draw' was protecting. Both the draw tool and the bootstrap seed numpy's default_rng from the recorded seed, and C-5 reproduces the draw from the manifest, so the beacon seed is handled exactly as a person's seed would be. What the beacon does NOT supply is a person. The charter's Q2 (DRAW-EXTERNAL) is defined mechanically in B-6 (drawn-by not in the excluded list, seed not 52525254 or 777, test set not GRV-BLIND-1's) and the frozen verdict script will render it from the manifest string; a beacon draw passes that test, but B-6 already says the identity behind the string is attestation and that the section 2b count is the author's to grant. The amendment therefore states that the Q2 form records independence of the seed, not a human adjudication, and that the 2b increment (1 to 2) stays the author's decision; the write-up must say 'beacon draw', never 'adjudicator'. Q1, the physics (does cH0/2pi hold at the 0.95-quantile rms bar on 30 fresh galaxies), is fully and honestly answerable this way, with the kept-failure clause intact. Withdrawing would discard a sub-0.1-CPU-hour confrontation of the programme's best D1 content for want of a five-minute human step that the autonomy rule says the lab must not wait on. Scope reminder from the charter itself: a pass establishes a blind procedure, not data novelty or the coefficient 1/2pi.

## Alternatives considered

- withdraw: rejected. The commission is not dishonest without a person; only the attestation behind the drawn-by string changes, and the charter already reserves the section 2b count to the author. The verdict forms, bars, floors and data are untouched, so no bar bends. Withdrawing would leave a cheap, target-facing confrontation of a D1 observable unrun for a reason the autonomy rule forbids.
- wait for a human adjudicator (leave the job BLOCKED): rejected. Rule 11 says no step may wait on a person; the driver has already refused three times and will refuse indefinitely.
- have the lab or the author draw (e.g. drawn-by 'lab' or 'author'): rejected. The seed would be chosen by a party the hold-out discipline (section W) is meant to exclude, and B-6 would correctly render DRAW-NOT-EXTERNAL; it would also repeat GRV-BLIND-1's author's-draw situation and move nothing on section 2b.
