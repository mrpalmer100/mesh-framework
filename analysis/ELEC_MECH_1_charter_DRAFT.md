# COMMISSION ELEC-MECH-1 -- THE SECOND-MOMENT SCREEN: WHICH REGISTERED MECHANISM CLASSES CAN GIVE THE ELECTRON'S g = 2
# (CHARTER, the author's DRAFT for the lab's lock, 2026-10-25; not a candidate and not a census of configurations:
# a screen over MECHANISM CLASSES by the one dimensionless number every candidate must meet, computed from the
# registered mechanics of each class with no fitted parameter; Legs A and B on closed forms and existing
# instruments, Leg C gated on an instrument credential the executor delivers before the lock; harness on
# benchmarks/lab/_kit/labkit.py; the measured g, mu_e, alpha and the Schwinger term enter only the verdict's last line)

## WHERE IT STANDS (the honest sentence, before the question)
The electron sector contributes nothing at any prediction tier (NORTH_STAR scorecard; the "strength of magnets"
row is blocked by it). Every candidate has died on a wall the registry can name: the static clasp on size and
the electrostatic fraction (ELEC-021/036), the texture soliton on mass and scale (ELEC-075), the alpha chain's
terminus on its gyromagnetic ratio computed from its own motion (ELEC-102, G-OTHER: 1.82 and 1.11 under the two
charge readings), and this week the rotating twisted rope of the electron ledger's hypothesis H on the same ratio
(analysis/ELEC_E2_rotator_results.md section 7: g = 0.91 to 0.97 across two decades of twist stiffness, because at
fixed J the twist distributes like the energy and charge that follows mass gives g = 1 for any rigid rotator).
What those kills have in common is the ratio they fail, and that ratio has a structure: for any stationary
configuration in its rest frame whose charge density rho_q and energy density epsilon circulate about the spin
axis with local angular velocities omega_q(r) and omega_E(r),
    mu = (1 / 2) Int rho_q omega_q r_perp^2 dV,   J = (1 / c^2) Int epsilon omega_E r_perp^2 dV,   m = (1 / c^2) Int epsilon dV,
so that
    g = (mu / J)(2 m / q) = <omega_q r^2>_q / <omega_E r^2>_E,
the ratio of the charge's and the energy's angular-velocity-weighted second moments (ELEC-G-2's D1 is the rigid
case omega_q = omega_E). This number is the sharpest screen the sector has: it is dimensionless, it cannot be
fitted, the measured value is 2.0023, and the classical cases are a short list (co-moving charge and mass give 1;
charge on the rim of a mass with <r^2>_E = R^2 / 2 gives 2; the open string with end charge gives 2 because its
mass is spread along it). The question is which registered mechanism class can put the charge's moment at twice
the energy's without a parameter put there by hand. The author's framing, 2026-10-25: if the framework cannot find
the electron it has little value; this commission is the fastest honest way to learn whether any registered
mechanism can.

## THE QUESTION
  Q1  THE SCREEN. For each mechanism class M1 to M8 below, computed from the registered mechanics of the class
      with no fitted parameter: the ratio g_M = <omega_q r^2>_q / <omega_E r^2>_E, as a number or a closed form,
      and whether it can equal 2 (within the band of D3) at any point of the class's registered parameter space;
      if only at a tuned point, the tuned quantity named as the class's price.
  Q2  COMPLETENESS. Is the list M1 to M8 the whole constructible space of charge-and-energy circulations the
      registered medium admits (the GRV-013 standard: every direction of escape named and closed, or named and
      left open as a fence test), stated as a theorem-grade argument or as a list of named gaps?
  Q3  THE SURVIVOR'S NEXT WALL (a table, not a verdict): for any class that reaches 2, the size and mass it would
      imply at J = hbar / 2 and m = m_e, beside ELEC-036's form-factor bar and W3's tension ladder, so the author
      sees the next wall before chartering the candidate. For M7b the table is already computable in closed form
      (kappa = hbar c / 4 pi, T = m_e^2 c^3 / 4 pi hbar, R = lambda-bar_C, against the registered kappa / T) and the
      commission renders it with the registered rope constants in it, not the executor's estimate.

## THE CLASSES (fixed here; Q2 tests the list, no class is added after the lock)
  M1  Rigid rotator, closed rope, charge = the twist density co-moving with the rope (E2b as built): the Routhian
      puts the twist at the folds like the energy; g = 1 up to the Coulomb spreading. ON THE RECORD (0.91 to 0.97).
  M2  Rigid rotator, closed rope, any charge locus tied to the energy density by a registered rule (uniform in

  M3  The alpha chain's terminus (ELEC-102's two readings): 1.82 and 1.11. ON THE RECORD. Whether a THIRD reading
      exists that the registry selects is Q2's business, not a new reading invented here.
  M4  Open rope with the charge at a lightlike end (ledger section 6's arithmetic): g = 2 exactly, at the price of
      ENDS, which are not registered objects (the medium's strands are closed or anchored, FND-MATTER-004 /
      GRV-020). Scored as REACHES-2-AT-A-PRICE with the price named; whether an end can be a registered object is
      a grant question for the author, out of scope here.
  M5  A lightlike circulating pulse carrying the charge and ALL the energy (the zitterbewegung picture with no
      rope tension counted in m): charge and energy co-located, g = 1 by the formula. Closed form. (The same pulse
      WITH the rope's tension counted is M7b, a different class with a different answer.)
  M6  The texture soliton (ELEC-074's hollow object) in rigid rotation with the charge at its boundary or
      profile-weighted: the same computation as ELEC-102 on ELEC-074's profile p(x); already dead as the electron
      by ELEC-075 but scored for completeness of the screen.
  M7  NON-RIGID: the twist pattern moving along the rope relative to its material, the pattern's own momentum
      density (energy flux over c^2, EM-019) counted in J, the transported winding (EM-008 / EM-014) as the
      current. Two sub-classes, decided by the pattern speed beta_p c relative to the material:
      M7a beta_p = 0, the co-moving twist (E2b): g = 0.91 to 0.97. ON THE RECORD.
      M7b beta_p = 1, the torsion wave at light speed (EM-RECON-012) on the closed rope: the executor's closed
          form and instrument (analysis/ELEC_MECH_1_legC_instrument.md) give a static ring with R = sqrt(kappa / T),
          E_tension = E_wave by a virial, J = 2 pi kappa / c quantized by the winding, and g = 2 EXACTLY, nothing
          tuned (2.0034 by free minimization at M = 48). Its prices, to be written on the form's face: 9 percent
          above M7a in energy at equal kappa and J (stability), and kappa / T = lambda-bar_C^2 required against the
          registered rope's standoff-squared (nine orders, A7). Leg C scans beta_p between 0 and 1 and both seeds
          at the ring's own J and at J = pi; the forms are decided by the band and the price rule, not by the
          executor's numbers, which are the credential's.
  M8  INTRINSIC: spin carried by the internal azimuth itself (the registered spin machinery, ELEC-100: QB-025's
      half-angle law, QB-031's transport law, GRV-020's Goldstone), i.e. the strand pair rotating about the rope's
      own centreline at rate phi-dot with moment of inertia I_pair and the charge on the strands at the standoff
      d0 / 2: J_int = Int I_pair phi-dot ds, mu_int from the strands' circulation about the centreline; g from the
      pair's registered geometry (EM-RECON-017/018 standoff, the strand mass per length mu = T / c^2, FND-006).
      Closed form in the rigid-pair limit; COMPUTED in Leg C if the pair's internal motion is not rigid.

## WHAT IS NOT ASSUMED, AND WHAT IS FORBIDDEN
No charge locus is chosen to make a number come out; every locus is the one a registered rule gives, and a class
whose locus the registry does not fix is scored under each registered reading with the readings named. No
parameter is tuned toward 2; a class that reaches 2 only at a tuned value is REACHES-2-AT-A-PRICE with the value
named, never a survivor. The measured g, mu_e, the Schwinger term and alpha are forbidden to the driver; the
verdict loads 2 and 2.0023193 in its last unit. Nothing about which class survives is predicted by the author,
the executor or the lab.

## SCOPE (hard boundary)
g only. Size, mass, the form factor, the tension ladder and stability are Q3's table for a survivor and the next
charter's question; this commission kills or passes classes on g alone. No new configuration is built; the
instrument of Leg C is a computation on the registered rope, not a candidate. The spinor route (g = 2 as a
non-classical moment ratio) is out of scope as a mechanism, because the registry has no mechanics for it; Q2
names it as a direction of escape, open.

## DESIGN COMMITMENTS (locked at lock)
D1. THE STATISTIC: g_M as defined above, computed per class from the class's registered densities and angular
    velocities; for rigid classes <r^2>_q / <r^2>_E on the configuration (ELEC-G-2's D1); for M7 and M8 from the
    instrument's sealed arrays (charge density, energy density, pattern and material angular velocities, the
    pattern's momentum density).
D2. CONVERGENCE for computed classes: the ratio stable to 1e-3 relative across the instrument's two finest
    resolutions and across the parameter scan's endpoints where the class has a parameter (kappa_tw for M7 over
    0.01 to 1; the standoff over EM-RECON-018's band for M8), else NO-CALL for that class.
D3. THE BAND: a class REACHES 2 if |g_M - 2| <= 1e-2 somewhere in its registered parameter space with no tuned
    quantity (1e-2 is coarse on purpose: the screen asks "can this class get there", not "is it 2.0023"; a
    survivor's precise value is the successor's question). REACHES-AT-A-PRICE if only at a tuned value.
D4. COMPLETENESS (Q2): the argument is written before Leg C runs and sealed with the credential; its named
    gaps are forms, not footnotes.

## FORMS (exhaustive; pre-registered; per class then overall)
Per class (exactly one each): REACHES-2 (D3, no price); REACHES-2-AT-A-PRICE (the tuned quantity named);

  SCREEN-SURVIVOR     at least one class REACHES-2 with no price: named; Q3's table rendered for it; the next
                      charter is that class's candidate against its next wall.
  SCREEN-PRICED       no class reaches 2 unpriced, at least one at a price: the prices listed (ends; a charge
                      locus the registry does not fix; a pattern speed above the screw's) as the grant questions
                      the author must decide before any electron candidate is chartered.
  SCREEN-NONE         every class FAILS-2 and the completeness argument closes every named gap: no registered
                      mechanism can give the electron's g; the sector needs a new primitive, and this record is
                      the price sheet's first line. Kept as the result it is.
  SCREEN-OPEN         the completeness argument leaves a named gap that no class covers (for instance the spinor
                      route): the screen's negative is scoped to the classes, and the gap is the next charter.
  INSTRUMENT-OPEN     Leg C's credential unmet; Legs A and B render, M7 and M8 are NO-CALL, the overall form is
                      SCREEN-OPEN with the instrument named as the gap.
  NO-CALL             v1 or v2 unmet.
