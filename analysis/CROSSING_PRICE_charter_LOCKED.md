## The three derivations, stated before computing

  D1  A_c, the crossing overlap energy. The registered contact is a soft interpenetration, finite
      at zero separation (FND-KIN-005: contact is finite in form; FND-MATTER-004: tangibility is
      density energetics). The registered form A_c/(1 + (r/sigma)^4) (EM-RECON-023) has the
      shape; EM-RECON-017/018 price compressing contacting strands BY the contact energy under
      P-VOL (volume conservation) and derive the standoff band r/sigma in [0.40, 0.46]; EM-RECON-009
      ties the quartic coefficient of the density-to-energy map to the stretch modulus,
      c4 = (k - T0)/8 exactly, k = 2 T0. D1 asks: does the registered density-to-energy map, with
      k = 2 T0 and P-VOL, fix the energy of two coarse strands crossing at the registered standoff,
      in units of T0 a (tension energy per spacing)? If yes, A_c = (that number) x T0 a at each
      scale set. If the map fixes only the shape and its amplitude is a calibration, D1 says so
      and names the calibration (the fence prompt permits exactly one across the programme).
  D2  sigma, the contact range. EM-RECON-017's P-VOL kinematics give sigma(eps) = sigma_0/sqrt(1+eps)
      with sigma_0 scaling as the strand width; the strand width is the registered thickness d_c
      (HBAR-005) under the rod class (FND-118). D2 asks whether the registry fixes sigma_0/d_c as a
      number (the standoff band and the registered strand geometry together), or only brackets it.
  D3  Crossings per node. The coarse weave's geometry: FND-091's derived angles (psi_1, psi_2),
      the pitch regime, and the cell (kappa_pack strands per cell at each scale set). D3 asks for
      the number of crossings a strand makes per spacing a, as a derivation from those, and the
      pressing at each (the standoff band of EM-RECON-018 applies if the weave sits at it; D3 says
      whether it does).
  D4  Close the chain: V_sec per crossing from T-1's V_sec/V_line at rho/sigma (rho = d_c/2),
      V_0 = V_sec x crossings per node, then w, omega_min and E_c at the three scale sets,
      confronted with the laser record and Schwinger. D4 runs only if D1 to D3 each return a
      registered-input number; otherwise the chain is written with the gap named.
