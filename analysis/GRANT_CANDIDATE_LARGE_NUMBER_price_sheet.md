# GRANT-CANDIDATE-LARGE-NUMBER -- the count behind hbar, priced for the author's desk

## Why a candidate exists

ACTION-BRIDGE (FND-183) reduced the one fence's Task 1 to a single demand. In this framework
hbar and the derived snap action A* are both tension times area over c, their ratio N = hbar/A*
carries one inverse power of the lattice spacing, and the postulate set {T0, a, c, Pi} has a
full-rank dimension matrix, so its only action is T0 a^2 g(Pi)/c. Deriving hbar therefore means
deriving a pure number g(Pi) that equals N at the true spacing, and the spacing is then fixed by
the result: Task 1 and Task 2 are one equation. The number is on the record: 1.0e21 to 2.0e22 at
the adopted Planck-class spacing (F-SAK), 2.2e3 to 4.2e4 at the Lorentz-bound spacing (F-LOR,
two registered conventions). Nothing registered produces a pure number of either size from Pi,
and the registry does not hold Pi's own value. This sheet prices the slot that would.

## The candidate, stated in full (a slot with its acceptance test, not a mechanism)

  GRANT-CANDIDATE-LARGE-NUMBER: there exists a mechanism of the weave, built from the postulates
  alone, that fixes a dimensionless count g(Pi) such that the medium's quantum of action is
  hbar = g(Pi) A* = g(Pi) (3 beta/(0.23 chi)) T0 a h / c. The mechanism must (i) consume no
  measured constant (hbar, alpha, l_q, m_e, G), (ii) produce g as a derivation with its
  a-dependence read by machine (g must be a^0 for the equation to pin a; a g that carries a
  power of a is a different claim and must say so), and (iii) be stated with its falsifier
  before g is evaluated.

This is NOT a fourth imported sentence; it is the specification of what a fourth sentence would
have to do. The author adopts a mechanism, if ever, not this sheet. Until then Grant 3 ("the
quantum arrives whole") carries hbar as an import and the sheet records what retiring it costs.

## What it is and is not

  It IS the statement of an acceptance test for any future mechanism claiming to derive hbar.
  It is NOT a mechanism, not a sentence, not a grant; it cannot be adopted, only met.
  It does NOT promise that such a mechanism exists. ACTION-BRIDGE's S4 says the postulates
  alone cannot produce a fork-invariant action; a mechanism meeting this sheet would do so by
  producing a number, not a length, which S4 permits and the enumeration did not find.
  It touches nothing registered until a mechanism is adopted.

## The price (of adopting any mechanism that meets the test)

  P1. ONE NEW MECHANISM CLASS, named by whoever proposes it (a coherence count, a percolation
      inverse, an exponential of 1/Pi, a combinatorial count over the weave's states).
  P2. PI'S VALUE becomes load-bearing: the corpus has never needed Pi as a number; a g(Pi) of
      order 1e21 would need Pi to about one part in 1e3 if g is exponential, or to a few percent
      if g is a high power. Registering Pi is then a demand, and Pi has no registered route.
  P3. THE FORK IS DECIDED BY THE RESULT: a landing fixes a, which must then agree with R2's
      Planck-class selection within sqrt(D) (test d), or the postulate set contradicts itself
      (ACTION-BRIDGE's BRIDGE-SCALE-TENSION form). The price of success is a confrontation.
  P4. EVERY hbar-CONSUMING CLAIM acquires a provenance rider (hbar now derived; the imported
      value becomes a check), and Grant 3's constant retires. This is a large re-read, done once.

## Falsifiers (for any proposed mechanism, pre-registered here)

  F1. The mechanism's count consumes hbar, alpha, l_q, m_e or G at any step (B-1 of
      ACTION-BRIDGE): not a derivation.
  F2. The count carries a nonzero power of a when the invariants are held fixed: it is not a
      pure number and cannot pin the spacing; it is a different claim.
  F3. The count is adjusted to land (a choice among brackets, a fitted Pi): coincidence, not
      bridge (bar B-2).
  F4. The count lands, pins a, and the pinned a lies outside R2's band: the postulate set fixes
      the scale twice and disagrees. Registered as a tension; the mechanism is kept as a failure.
  F5. The count lands and pins a inside R2's band, but the derived hbar fails pi^2/240 (the
      Casimir coefficient with the derived constant): kills the bridge at its first cash-out.
