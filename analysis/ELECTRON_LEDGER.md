## 0. Executive summary

The registry has derived what the electron's charge is and how its spin transports, and it has failed, by its
own kept results, to make the committed configuration (the two-strand clasp) into an object with the electron's
mass, size, stability or magnetic moment. Five independent kills are on the record: no stationary static clasp
was ever reached (ELEC-035); the form factor misses by four orders and the two anchors overdetermine it (ELEC-036,
ELEC-037); a static soliton does not exist (ELEC-068); the dynamical stabilization that rescued it in one
dimension is a one-dimensional artifact (ELEC-070); the structural soliton that survives is too light by 1e37 if
sized right and a micron across if massed right (ELEC-075). The alpha chain's rotating terminus is a
two-dimensional effective functional with no registered mechanical reading (the reviewers' finding on
ELEC-CURRENT-1), and its second moments do not give Dirac's g (ELEC-102).

One reading of the electron has not been tested, and the registry's own conjecture ELEC-066 points at it: an
object whose extent is set by its own motion rather than by the strand spacing. Section 6 states it in the
sharpest form the arithmetic allows. A string of ANY tension rotating with its ends at the speed of light obeys
R = 2 c J / E; with the electron's spin and mass as inputs that is R = hbar / (m_e c), the reduced Compton
wavelength, with no freedom; a unit charge at the lightlike end gives g = 2 exactly, for any tension and radius;
and the tension that carries an object of spin hbar / 2 and mass m_e is fixed, T_eff = m_e^2 c^3 / (pi hbar) =
0.0675 J/m, which is 1 / 6431 of the registered coarse tension and about 1e6 of the registered fine sub-strands.
The earlier kill of "dynamical stabilization" (ELEC-070) was at fixed internal frequency; at fixed angular
momentum the rotation is repulsive at short scale (E = A L + K / L^3) and stabilizes in three dimensions. None of
this derives hbar, the lepton generations, or the measured form factor; it converts those into three named tests
with no fitted parameter. Section 7 locks what any candidate must meet; section 8 says what E2 and E3 are.

## 2. The static clasp: what it is, what it got right, how it died

The committed configuration is two closed strand curves linked once (a Hopf link, `hopf_curves` deformed by
Fourier modes, ELEC-020's state), minimized under a curve-field functional (tension along the curves, Poisson
coupling of strength kappa between them). It is the minimal charge-one object on the registry's own topology:
a winding line in a circle-valued field cannot end, so charge one closes as two linked loops (GG-006 makes the
linking number the handedness). That part is right and survives everything below.

What was attached to it is a STATIC reading: the mass is the configuration's binding energy (46 percent tension,
54 percent electrostatic at the calibration, ELEC-021), the size is the configuration's extent, and the two are
tied by the anchors E = m_e c^2 and kappa = alpha hbar c. The consequences are on the record:
- The optimizer never reached a stationary point: 35 sessions of descent, three metrics, a Clarke test; "not
  stationary in any sense" (ELEC-035). The ladder's energy E_inf = 15.5627 (ELEC-020) is the value at the
  terminus of a converging method, not of a solution.
- The size: with both anchors the rms charge radius is 21.16 fm against structure bounds near 1e-3 fm, a miss of
  2.1e4, and branches A and B carry the SAME factor because the anchors fix exactly one length (ELEC-036, ELEC-037
  shows the no-go is scale-invariant). This is the classical electron radius, alpha hbar c / (m_e c^2) = 2.82 fm,
  with the clasp's geometric factor on it: any configuration whose mass is its static self-energy at coupling
  alpha lands here. The clasp did not fail because it is a clasp; it failed because it is static.
- Derrick: no static localized solution exists for the pure-tension functional in any dimension (ELEC-068 B2).
So the static clasp is dead three ways, and the record already says the surviving shape is "dynamical without
long-range coordination, a local circulation whose extent is set by the medium rather than by signal reach"
(ELEC-066, filed as a conjecture with no computation behind it).

## 3. The dynamical line (ELEC-066 to ELEC-076): what was tested, and what was not

The line that followed ELEC-066 tested a transverse-displacement soliton psi(x) with an internal oscillation at
frequency omega (a breather), under the quartic-plus-sextic expansion of the strain energy:
- In 1+1 dimensions the internal-frequency term stabilizes the attractive quartic and gives L* = c / omega
  (ELEC-068), the window is reachable but shallow (ELEC-069).
- In 3+1 the same term scales as +C L^3 under psi(x) -> psi(x / L) at FIXED omega, so E = A L - B / L + C L^3 is
  monotone and the dynamical stabilization is a one-dimensional artifact; L* = c / omega lapses (ELEC-070).
- A positive sextic stabilizes structurally instead (ELEC-070 B3), for both signs of the quartic (ELEC-072), but
  the exact hollow object it gives is the continuum failing inside one strand spacing and is 1e37 too light at
  the size bound (ELEC-074, ELEC-075); and the line was found to carry two functionals disagreeing by eight
  orders, with the question "does the medium shorten at constant tension or resist with stiffness k" left open
  (ELEC-076).

What that line did NOT test is the other ensemble. ELEC-070 varied the width at fixed frequency. An object with
fixed ANGULAR MOMENTUM, the electron's actual constraint (its spin is hbar / 2 whatever its size), scales the
opposite way: a rotating object of size L made of strands has energy A L from tension and moment of inertia
proportional to (mass) x L^2 = (T L / c^2) L^2, so at fixed J the rotational energy is J^2 / (2 I) = K / L^3,
REPULSIVE at short scale, and E = A L + K / L^3 has a genuine minimum at L* = (3 K / A)^(1/4) in three dimensions
with no sextic and no attractive quartic needed. Derrick's argument at fixed omega and at fixed J are different
statements; the registry ran the first. The second is the content of section 6.

## 4. The alpha chain's terminus, and ELEC-102

The alpha chain (Commissions T to Z) modelled the electron as "a rotating winding terminus": the EL-BVP solution
of explorations/w_dressing_phase1c.py, a radial profile f(r) on a disk of radius x* = e^{pi^2} rotating at
Omega = pi / x*, with the normalization J = J_T imposed, from which 1 / alpha = 4 pi^3 D_E lands at +179 ppm with
no continuous dial, and "rotation, not energy, sets the scale" (R* = pi lambda-bar_C). Its Gate 2b reported a
"mechanical" moment of pi mu_B with the charge at the edge, converted to 1 mu_B (Dirac) by the Gate-1 recording
factor 1 / pi, with two flags on its face and restraint in its wording.

ELEC-G-2 (ELEC-102, provisional) computed the terminus's charge-to-energy second-moment ratio with no imported
factor: 1.8209 (charge at the edge) and 1.1149 (charge following the profile), neither 2 nor 1 nor 2 pi. Its
write-up recorded two facts the ledger carries forward: (i) Omega x* = pi in units of c, so the terminus's edge
would move at pi c under rigid rotation, which is why the "pi mu_B" appears (mu = q v R / 2 with v = pi c gives
pi mu_B; the 1 / pi "recording factor" is the correction that puts the edge back at c); (ii) the ratio is a
gyromagnetic ratio only under current and momentum laws (CL1, CL2) the registry does not hold for this object,
and the ELEC-CURRENT-1 reviews then established that no such laws CAN be derived for it, because f(r) has no
registered reading as a configuration of strands (R10 fails). The terminus's numbers (D_E, R*) are therefore
Modeled results of an effective functional; they are not evidence about a mechanical electron until the
functional is derived from one.

## 5. The walls any candidate must clear (registered, not negotiable)

- W1, the causal budget (ELEC-043): transverse waves travel at exactly c, so only strands within c tau can
  participate in a process of duration tau. A candidate may not rely on coordination faster than that. A rigidly
  rotating object of size R at period 2 pi R / c coordinates by tension, within its own light-crossing time;
  that is inside the budget, but it must be shown, not assumed.
- W2, the one-medium declaration (ELEC-038): the vacuum strands and the matter strands are one medium; no second
  medium with its own tension is available (ELEC-060 priced the two-medium escape and it rescues the electron
  only).
- W3, the strand scale (ELEC-053, FND-MATTER-044, FND-110): a = 6.0e-17 m, T0 = 434 J/m at the M-point, w =
  a / sqrt 3; the fine level T0_f = T0 / n_sub with n_sub in [4.6e9, 1.3e10] (FND-083, FND-087), suspended on
  n_sub. A candidate's tension must be one of these or a registered multiple (tube tension is additive,
  QGATE-005).
- W4, no material points (FND-REL-002, ELEC-077): there is no material velocity; a circulation is a phase or a
  pattern, and its momentum is the inertial term's (EM-019). "The charge moves at c" means the winding's phase
  pattern moves at c, which a transverse wave does; it does not mean matter moves.
- W5, the form-factor bound is a bound on what a PROBE sees (ELEC-036 branch C, ELEC-066's point): a static
  charge cloud of radius R scatters as a cloud of radius R; a circulating charge at c is a different scattering
  problem, and the Dirac electron itself has a zitterbewegung extent of lambda-bar_C while scattering as a point.
  A candidate of Compton size does not pass R6 by assertion; it owes the computation.
- W6, no fitted number: hbar, m_e, alpha and e are inputs; a candidate may use them as inputs and must say which
  it used; a number it then predicts must be one it did not use.

## 6. The hypothesis the ledger puts forward: the rotating charge-one pair at fixed angular momentum

Stated as a hypothesis, H: the electron is the charge-one linked pair (the clasp's topology, R1) in steady
rotation, its mass the energy of the rotating tensioned strands, its spin the angular momentum of that rotation,
its charge-bearing winding at the lightlike edge, and its size whatever those three fix. The static anchors of
ELEC-021 (mass = binding energy; size = configuration extent) are retired under H; the topology is kept.

What the arithmetic of a rotating string gives, before any solver is built (benchmarks/foundations/
electron_ledger_numbers.py reproduces every number):
- A string of tension T rotating rigidly with its ends at c has E = pi T R and J = pi T R^2 / (2 c), hence
  J = E^2 / (2 pi T c) (the Regge relation) and, for ANY tension, R = 2 c J / E.
- With J = hbar / 2 and E = m_e c^2 as inputs: R = hbar / (m_e c) = 3.8616e-13 m, the reduced Compton wavelength,
  exactly and with no freedom. R9 is met by identity, not by fit. The rotation period is 2 pi R / c = 8.09e-21 s,
  frequency 1.24e20 Hz, half the Dirac zitterbewegung frequency 2 m c^2 / h (a known feature of the Regge
  rotator relative to the Dirac zbw; the factor two is the first place the candidate can be wrong and the first
  thing E3 should measure).
- A unit charge at the lightlike end: mu = q c R / 2, and g = (mu / J)(2 m / q) = 2 exactly, for any T and any R.
  R4's Dirac value is a theorem of the geometry, not a byproduct with a transferred factor. (The alpha chain's
  pi mu_B is this same rotator with its edge at pi c; put the edge at c and the factor is gone. That is what Gate
  2b's "1 / pi" was doing.) The radiative 0.0023 is not in the geometry; it is a test for the dressing (D_E),
  not for H.
- The tension that carries spin hbar / 2 at mass m_e is fixed: T_eff = m_e^2 c^3 / (pi hbar) = 6.7486e-2 J/m.
  That is 1 / 6431 of the coarse strand tension T0 = 434 J/m, and 7.2e5 to 2.0e6 fine sub-strands at the priced
  n_sub. The electron would be a rotating bundle of order 1e6 fine strands, or a coarse strand carrying 1 / 6431
  of its tension; the registry holds neither number, and this is H's PRICE: one registered tension must equal
  T_eff, or H is wrong.
- The electrostatic self-energy of a unit charge at scale R is alpha hbar c / R = alpha m_e c^2, 0.73 percent of
  the mass. Under H the mass is tension energy and the Coulomb part is a correction of order alpha, which is the
  opposite of the static clasp (54 percent electrostatic) and is what makes the classical-radius wall irrelevant.
- The Regge relation is also H's sharpest exposure: on the electron's tension, a spin-1/2 object of the muon's
  mass would need J = 4.3e4 hbar / 2, so the muon cannot be the same object on the same tension; it would need
  T = 2.9e3 J/m (6.6 T0) and the tau 8.2e5 J/m. H does not explain the generations; it predicts that if they are
  rotators they sit on different tensions, which is either a tube-count statement (QGATE-005 additivity) or a
  kill.

What H does and does not do against the table:
R1 kept. R2 kept (transport). R3: NOT derived; hbar / 2 is an input and the quantization of J is exactly HBAR-003's
open problem, now attached to an object. R4: g = 2 derived by geometry, conditional on H. R5: m_e becomes
T_eff, an input of a different kind (a tension in the registered ladder, or not). R6: owed, by computation (W5).
R7: stability at fixed J by scaling is shown above; shape and splitting stability are owed (ELEC-072 B4's
caveat applies). R8: untouched. R9: met by identity. R10: met in principle (a rotating pair of strand curves is a
mechanical state), owed in practice by E2.

What H retires if adopted: ELEC-021's calibration as the electron's anchor (the 15.5627 energy and its 46 / 54
partition become properties of a static configuration that is not the electron); the alpha chain's R* = pi
lambda-bar_C as a derived scale (it becomes the rotator's R with the edge mis-set by pi; the chain's D_E and the
178.8 ppm residual are to be re-derived on the rotating pair or stand as Modeled). What H does not touch: the
photon sector, the gravity sector, the nuclear mass table, Tsirelson, the transport law.

## 7. Acceptance criteria for ANY electron candidate (fixed here, before E2 exists; numeric where a number exists)

A candidate configuration C of the registered medium is scored on the following, each with its bar, and the
scoring commission's forms are built from these and nothing else:
  A1 Charge: winding one (R1), by the registered count; exactly.
  A2 A stationary, linearly stable state under the registered functional (pure tension, ELEC-077; the inertial
     term, EM-019; the Poisson coupling at kappa = alpha hbar c) in three dimensions, at FIXED angular momentum:
     residual below 1e-8 of the energy scale and no negative Hessian direction other than the symmetry modes.
  A3 Size: R_rms(charge) / (2 c J / E) within 10 percent of 1 (the rotator identity; a candidate that is not a
     rotator must state what sets its size instead).
  A4 The ratio g = (mu / J)(2 m / q) computed from the configuration's own current (transported linking of the
     moving winding, EM-008 / EM-014) and momentum density (EM-019, tension over c^2 times transverse velocity),
     with no imported factor: within 1e-3 of 2.
  A5 The electrostatic fraction E_Coulomb / E within a factor 2 of alpha (W5's consequence under H; a static
     candidate shows 0.5 and fails).
  A6 The edge speed: the winding's phase speed at the charge-bearing locus within 1 percent of c (W4: a pattern,
     not matter).
  A7 The tension: the candidate's T equals a registered tension or a registered multiple (W3) to 10 percent; if
     not, the mismatch is the kill and is registered as such.
  A8 Causality (W1): no coordination beyond c tau; shown by the solver's locality (the equations are local).
  A9 The form factor: computed by scattering a transverse wave off the rotating configuration (E4), not assumed;
     the bar is ELEC-036's 1e-3 fm on the EFFECTIVE scattering radius, with the Dirac zbw size as the stated
     comparison.
  A10 Nothing in A1 to A9 fitted: hbar, m_e, alpha, e used as inputs are named; every other number is output.
A candidate that fails A2 or A7 is dead; one that passes A1 to A8 and has A9 pending is the programme's electron
candidate and the form factor is its next test; the first candidate to pass A9 at Compton scale is the first
thing in the electron sector that is not a kept failure.
