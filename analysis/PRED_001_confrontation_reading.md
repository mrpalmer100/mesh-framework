## 1. The prediction (PRED-001, Modeled, T3 by ELEC-064)

Sum m_nu = 58.47 meV, normal ordering, masses (0.374, 8.686, 49.415) meV, from the Brannen form
m_i = mu (1 + sqrt 2 cos(delta + 2 pi i / 3))^2 with the charged-lepton phase delta_c = 0.2222 rad fixed from
m_mu / m_e, the neutrino offset delta_nu = delta_c + pi / 12 (an INPUT, declared), and the scale from the measured
solar splitting. The registered falsifier: a confirmed inverted ordering, or a sum measured away from 58 to 59 meV.
The registered caveat: the prediction sits just above the normal-ordering floor, so a confirming bound is weak
evidence and only a killing bound is informative.

## 2. The judge, locked before the lookup

EXCLUDED if the most recent DESI BAO + CMB 95 percent upper bound on Sum m_nu in LambdaCDM is below 58.47 meV, or
if the mass ordering is established as inverted. ALIVE-TIGHT if that bound is above 58.47 and below 100 meV. ALIVE
otherwise. KATRIN's direct limit is reported and cannot decide (it sits far above the range). Executor's prior,
disclosed: the DESI DR2 LambdaCDM bound sits in the 60 to 70 meV range (ALIVE-TIGHT) and the DESI posterior prefers
smaller sums; if the current number has moved below 58.47, EXCLUDED stands.

## 3. The numbers (DESI collaboration, "Constraints on Neutrino Physics from DESI DR2 BAO and DR1 Full Shape",
arXiv:2503.14744, v3 of 7 October 2025)

- LambdaCDM, DESI DR2 BAO + CMB, marginalized posterior: Sum m_nu < 0.0642 eV at 95 percent (the headline number).
- The same data with the Feldman-Cousins construction, which corrects for the physical boundary at zero mass:
  Sum m_nu < 0.053 eV at 95 percent, which the paper itself describes as "breaching the lower limit from neutrino
  oscillations" (the normal-ordering floor, 0.059 eV); in a Bayesian analysis with an effective mass parameter the
  paper reports a 3 sigma tension with that floor.
- w0waCDM (dynamical dark energy): Sum m_nu < 0.163 eV at 95 percent, which the paper says "relaxes the neutrino
  tension."
- KATRIN direct limit (2025): m_beta < 0.45 eV at 90 percent; not decisive, as pre-stated.
- Ordering: not established by any measurement as of this reading.

## 4. Verdict under the locked judge, with the lock's one ambiguity stated

The lock named "the 95 percent upper bound in LambdaCDM" without naming the statistic, and the paper gives two under
LambdaCDM. Under the headline marginalized bound (64.2 meV) PRED-001 is ALIVE-TIGHT: the prediction sits 5.7 meV
below the bound, as the executor's prior said. Under the Feldman-Cousins bound (53 meV) it is EXCLUDED at 95
percent, together with the entire normal-ordering floor, which is the "negative neutrino mass" tension the field
has carried since DESI DR1 and which DESI itself reports at 3 sigma. Both are recorded; the lock's failure to name
the statistic is the executor's, and the honest verdict is: ALIVE-TIGHT on the paper's headline, EXCLUDED on its
boundary-corrected statistic, and in either case the prediction is where the registered caveat put it, at the
floor, with no room to move.

What the reading does NOT do: it does not choose w0waCDM to rescue the prediction. It records, because it was
written the same day and before this lookup, that analysis/SIGMA_LAMBDA_reading.md found the mesh's cosmological
term to be of the relaxation kind (not exactly w = -1), which would place a mesh cosmology in the dynamical class
whose bound is 163 meV. That is a statement about which bound a mesh cosmology would face, made before the number
was seen; it is not a reason to prefer that bound now, and under the house rules the LambdaCDM judge stands as
locked.
