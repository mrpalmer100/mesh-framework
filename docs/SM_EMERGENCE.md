## The tally

Grades D0 to D3 are defined in NORTH_STAR section 2c; a row is Derived here only at D0 or D1. A row is Derived when the registry holds a theorem or a code-backed derivation of the
structure itself from the postulates (ontology.md, A1 and A2, plus the registered inputs of
NORTH_STAR section 3). It is Partial when the registry holds the form, the kinematics or a
necessary ingredient but not the structure, or holds it only with an imported piece. It is
Unexplained when nothing registered addresses it, when every attempt is Failed and kept, or
when it enters as an axiom. A title is not a verdict; every row cites what it rests on.

## Reading the tally

The one Derived row is the one the programme was built around: electromagnetism as the
geometry of a weave. The four Partial rows share a shape: the mesh holds the kinematics
(Lorentz-covariant waves, integer windings, two-state response objects, the square in Born's
rule) and lacks the dynamics or the carrier (hbar, the electron, a fermion, the guidance
flow). The seven Unexplained rows are the Standard Model's gauge structure beyond U(1), its
statistics, its generations and its numbers. Every one of the Partial rows, and rows 6, 10
and 11 among the Unexplained, stops at the same place the North Star calls the fence: the
quantum layer. Rows 7, 8 and 9 stop earlier, at scope: the programme has not claimed them.

GAUGE-INVENTORY (FND-185, 4 October 2026) gave rows 7 and 8 their registered reason without moving them: the weave's continuous internal space has one real dimension (the U(1) of row 3) and the groups need three to eight; what the weave has beyond that is finite. The tally is unchanged, and the gap is now a count rather than an absence.

This page does not move by effort spent or claims registered. It moves when a row's status
changes by a registered derivation, and the release note says which row and why. A release
that leaves all twelve rows where they were is infrastructure, however many claims it adds.
