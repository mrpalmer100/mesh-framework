## The one-paragraph version

The programme's nuclear mass model was scored on isotopes it had never been inspected against,
twice: fifty drawn by the author under a seed he chose, and fifty drawn by a bystander at a
conference who typed his own seed into the author's machine. Both pre-registered models passed
both draws inside the bars locked before either draw was read (the three-term NUC-018 form at
0.12 to 0.14 percent of mass rms, the four-term form with the derived asymmetry coefficient
a_A = 19.85 MeV at 0.011 to 0.016 percent), with the residual of the three-term form shown to be
exactly the asymmetry term it declares omitted. By the bar the programme adopted from its own
external review four days earlier, the second draw is its first externally adjudicated prediction,
and the fifth of the five numbers moves from 0 to 1. The same four days drew the map the review
asked for: a twenty-by-thirteen closure matrix of every remaining primitive against every closure
target, joined by identity and exponent edges only, which finds five load-bearing gaps (the action
scale, the orientation well V_0, the internal dimension, the electron model, the proton topology)
and says which claims price each; a gauge inventory that finds the registered weave's only
continuous internal symmetry is the SO(2) already spent on electromagnetism, so the Standard
Model's non-abelian structure needs an internal space the registry does not yet have; a chain of
five sandbox commissions from the action bridge to the junction that traces hbar's bridge, the
strand mass scale and the charge-creation field to one object, a strand's face at a node, which
the registered vacuum does not touch, moving FND-182 from prediction to constraint. On the PC the
anti-aligned lineage closed: with the instrument repaired to certify a known-clean control, every
state of the registered anti-aligned march carries Nyquist content ten times the bar, so FND-174's
continuum reading is refuted and kept, and the torus-knot ladder meant to measure the
mass-versus-crossing law stopped at the solver's reach (n = 5 under a linear budget rule), an
instrument result now being repaired under a convergence rule. The registry's 121 Derived claims
were graded D0 to D3 and the grade column is on the North Star page; a scanner now lists every
measured constant each benchmark reads, with its 22 circularity flags adjudicated and the strict
check passing.
