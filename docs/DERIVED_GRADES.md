# Grades of the Derived claims (D0 to D3; NORTH_STAR section 2c)

*Reading commission REGRADE, 7 October 2026, on the author's word. Every claim with status Derived in
claims.yaml (121 at registry 779) graded against the rubric of NORTH_STAR 2c as written: D0 a theorem from
the axioms and registered definitions with no empirical input (a numerical result on the programme's own
engine is model-internal evidence and belongs here, not at D1); D1 a parameter-free observable confronted
with data from nature; D2 exact given a registered mesh parameter, import or adopted grant that is itself
unexplained; D3 a reconstruction from measured or calibrated quantities. The status field in claims.yaml
is unchanged; this page is the grade column. Machine-readable: analysis/derived_grades.json.*

## The tally

**95 D0, 2 D1, 19 D2, 2 D3, 3 not derivations (scope statements and historical slots).**

What the tally says: the programme's Derived column is almost entirely theorems and exact computations
internal to the model (D0), which is what a mechanical theory's foundations should look like and is not
evidence that nature is a mesh. Only two Derived claims meet nature with no parameter: CHEM-GEO-002 (the
heavy-hydride 90-degree asymptote, with two registered predictions outstanding) and GRV-031 (a
parameter-free galactic candidate that LOST its confrontation and was retired). The programme's strongest
D1 content is filed under Modeled, not Derived: GRV-030 (g_dagger = cH0/2pi on SPARC at zero parameters) and
PRED-003 (the drift ratio, pending adjudication). The grade and the status measure different things, and
the five numbers (NORTH_STAR 2b) count grades, not statuses.

## Status anomalies found by the reading (for the author)

- GRV-014 carries status Derived while its own title says CONJECTURE (admitted by audit). Graded D2.
- GRV-024 carries status Derived while its title says (Open): the exam was constructed and its verdict withheld. Graded D0 for the construction.
- GRV-036 and QB-024 carry status Derived while their titles say (Modeled). Graded D0 on content.
- NUC-001 is Derived and is a reconstruction "given measured binding energies" (its own words): D3. It is the base of the scorecard's "Weights of atoms" row, graded D3 there already.
