# The Mesh Programme v3.33.0 (8 Oct 2026) -- The first adjudicated confrontation

779 registered claims. The programme's nuclear mass model (one constant calibrated
on Ca-40; surface and Coulomb terms derived; the asymmetry coefficient derived at
19.85 MeV) was scored on isotopes it had never been inspected against, under a
hold-out bar adopted from external review: fifty drawn by the author and fifty
drawn by an adjudicator, both sealed before the models' predictions were read.
All four verdicts passed the pre-locked bars (0.12 to 0.14 and 0.011 to 0.016
percent of mass rms; NUC-BLIND-1/2), and the adjudicated draw is the programme's
first externally adjudicated prediction (NORTH_STAR 2b: 0 to 1). The same release
draws the gap map (CLOSURE-MATRIX, FND-186: five load-bearing gaps among twenty
primitives and thirteen targets), takes the gauge inventory (FND-185: the weave's
only continuous internal symmetry is the SO(2) already spent on electromagnetism),
closes the action-bridge chain at the junction (FND-183; FND-182 moved from
prediction to constraint), refutes the anti-aligned continuum reading under a
Nyquist bar and keeps it (FND-174), and registers the torus-knot solver's reach
(KNOT-REACH). Every Derived claim now carries a D0-D3 grade; every benchmark's
measured inputs are listed by a scanner with its circularity flags adjudicated.
